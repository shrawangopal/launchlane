# Third-party notices

Launchlane was built on the Sites Vinext starter. The starter's infrastructure scripts, UI primitives, example code, and existing license notices have been preserved. Original application code is under the repository's separate license notice.

- `build/sites-vite-plugin.ts` is vendored infrastructure; its MIT notice remains in `build/sites-vite-plugin.LICENSE`.
- React, Next/Vinext, Vite, Cloudflare tooling, Radix, Shadcn components, Lucide icons, Zod, Drizzle, Tailwind, and the remaining dependencies retain their upstream licenses. Exact versions are recorded in `package-lock.json`; dependency notices are distributed with installed packages.
- All bundled customer scenarios and scope documents were authored for this demonstration and are fictional.

This document identifies the major components; it is not a substitute for reviewing dependency licenses when redistributing a commercial build.
