# First paid pilot

## Proposed buyer and offer

Start with a service business onboarding several clients through documents and email. Sell a configured onboarding workflow with setup and ongoing support, rather than promising an autonomous replacement for an operations team.

Suggested opening message: “We turn your signed scope and onboarding checklist into one trackable client launch. Your client submits the required information, your team handles exceptions, and the handoff is built from accepted inputs.”

This positioning is a hypothesis. No customer demand, conversion rate, savings, or revenue has been validated for this project.

## Scope the first engagement

Pick one service, one operations owner, and one onboarding checklist. Map the actual obligations and acceptable evidence with that owner. Define who can approve inputs, what proves the client is ready, and which system receives the final handoff. Agree on a small paid implementation with a written acceptance checklist. Price only after learning the workload and integration/support effort.

## Demonstrate concrete value

- Compare median time from signed agreement to accepted handoff before and during the pilot.
- Record the operator's coordination time per launch, including reviewing agent output.
- Count missing-input follow-ups and handoffs reopened because information was wrong.
- Separate completed automated work from drafts, manual checks, and pending approvals.

Do not label time estimates as measured savings or treat a generated package as a live external drive integration.

## What this version delivers

Private operator workspace, three supported playbooks, client intake, validations, review/correction loop, evidence search, durable workflow state, internal workspace records, generated handoff/task/calendar artifacts, and a local filesystem materializer.

## Before processing real customer data

Confirm the deployment's authentication and client access path. The hosted preview is owner-private, so a link alone does not grant an external client access through the hosting gate. A commercial installation needs appropriate client access, identity, token expiration/revocation, workspace membership, backups, usage limits, privacy terms, and operational support. These are not represented as completed features.

Select and implement the first live connector against the pilot customer's actual tool. The repository intentionally does not claim Gmail, Drive, CRM, or calendar integration. Model-generated contract interpretation, OCR/PDF import, billing, and multi-user team management are also outside version 0.1.
